Hailing Diplomacy & Away-Team Adventures (Top Mode Tabs): Hail alien commanders (Tomalak, Duras, Gul Macet, the Crystalline Entity, or the Borg Queen) on the Main Viewscreen with live counsel from Troi, Data, and Worf.
