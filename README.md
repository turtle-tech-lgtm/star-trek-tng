# Star Trek: The Next Generation — USS Enterprise (NCC-1701-D) Command Simulator

### 🚀 [**▶ PLAY LIVE IN YOUR BROWSER (GitHub Pages)**](https://turtle-tech-lgtm.github.io/star-trek-tng/)
> **Direct Link:** [https://turtle-tech-lgtm.github.io/star-trek-tng/index.html](https://turtle-tech-lgtm.github.io/star-trek-tng/index.html)

An HTML5/Canvas/LCARS web game combining three classic *Star Trek: The Next Generation* gameplay modes:

1. **Bridge Commander Simulator:** Manage Matter/Antimatter Warp Power allocation across Weapons, Shields, Engines, and Sensors; reinforce 4 directional shield quadrants (`FWD`, `PORT`, `STBD`, `AFT`); tune your Shield/Phaser Harmonic Frequency (`240.0–260.0 MHz`); repair damaged subsystems; and trigger Senior Officer Bridge Abilities (**Picard Maneuver**, **Maneuver Omega**, **Modulate & Scan**, **Full Torpedo Spread**, **Reroute Warp Power**, **Empathic Probe**, and **Bio-Filter & Repair**).
2. **Hailing Diplomacy & Transporter Room 3 Away-Team Adventures:** Hail alien commanders (**Commander Tomalak**, **Captain Duras**, **The Crystalline Entity**, **Gul Macet**, **The Borg Collective**, and **The Borg Queen**) with live advisor insights from **Troi**, **Data**, and **Worf**, or beam a 4-officer Away Team down to the surface for branching narrative missions.
3. **Tactical Space Combat Arcade & 3-Phase Final Boss:** Pilot the Galaxy-class *USS Enterprise* (`NCC-1701-D`) in real-time 60fps tactical combat across 7 playable episodes—culminating in **Episode VII: The Queen's Nexus**, a 3-phase Final Boss battle against **The Borg Queen's Octahedral Command Diamond (`Unimatrix 01`)**.

## Running Locally

Open `index.html` directly in any modern browser, or serve locally:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8701
```

Then visit `http://localhost:8701`.
